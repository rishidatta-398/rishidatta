Now

What I'm working on

I hold two finance roles this year. My DECA chapter, which has 130 members, elected me Director of Finance. I run the chapter's finances and handle the paperwork behind every purchase we make. Separately, I'm one of five students who founded our Ethics Bowl club, where I track the finances and lead fundraising for a group that has grown to about fifteen members since we started it.

Both jobs are the unglamorous version of the thing I want to do professionally. Competitions reward a plan that looks good on paper. These roles involve real money, fixed constraints, and a lot of forms.

I'm also writing a research paper on credit risk and the way artificial intelligence is changing how it gets assessed, mentored by Lavanya Prahlad at Research Spark Hub. It is the first formal research I've done, and it asks a great deal more of me than a classroom assignment does.

Alongside it I'm preparing for my next DECA season after qualifying for the International Career Development Conference in Business Law and Ethics, and I've been accepted to both the Wharton Global Youth Summer Program and the Berkeley Haas BBAY Academy.
