Soccer

Ballistic United Soccer Club

01
Captain · Forward September 2018 — Present

Captain and top scorer

I have played for Ballistic United since 2018 and worn the armband for the last two seasons, with stretches of it before that. The job is not what I assumed it would be when I first got it. Scoring is the easy part. The actual work is handling the referee when a call goes against us, talking to the opposing captain when tempers rise, and keeping eleven people pointed the same direction when we're a goal down with twenty minutes left.

I am responsible for all communication with referees and opposing teams, and I'm expected to do it in a way that reflects well on the club whether or not the decision was fair. That constraint has taught me more about self-control than anything else I do.

  • Captained the club to first place in the NorCal Regional League, Fall 2025
  • Second place at the Ballistic Summer Classic tournament, twice
  • Second place at the Fremont Kick or Treat tournament, twice, both as captain
  • Led the team in scoring across league play and tournaments
  • Also played for the Amador Valley freshman team

Music

Percussion, on and off the field

02
Section Leader Amador Valley Honors Wind Ensemble · August 2024 — Present

Leading the percussion section

I lead the percussion section of the Honors Wind Ensemble, our audition band, and I was made a section leader in my first year in the marching band program, which does not usually happen. Percussion is the section where leadership is most physical: somebody has to know where every piece of equipment is, who is carrying it, and when it needs to be on the field.

I manage equipment and personnel for concerts, rehearsals, and travel. On trips that has meant taking responsibility for the whole section's gear across multi-day festivals.

  • Performed at the WBA Regional Championships
  • Performed at the Clovis Regional Festival
  • Performed at Sonoma State University
  • Performed at Disneyland
  • Member of the student led orchestra

Study & Performance Eleven years

Four traditions, one instrument family

I have studied drums for eight years under Lou Ruocco, working through a range of styles rather than settling into one. Outside of school I perform in cultural programs on djembe, tabla, and drumset, and I sing and dance in them as well. That has been going on for eleven years, longer than anything else in my life.

Playing tabla and playing a marching snare are almost unrelated skills, and moving between them has been the most useful musical training I've had. Each one exposes what the other lets you get away with.
