01Current

Elected office · Amador Valley DECA · 130 members

Director of Finance

My chapter has 130 members and they elected me to run its money. I organise the chapter's finances and complete the paperwork behind every purchase we make.

It is the least glamorous thing on this page and probably the most useful. Competitions reward a plan that looks good on paper, and I have spent two years getting good at those. This is the opposite exercise: the money is real, the constraints are fixed, and a large share of the work is filling out forms correctly so a purchase actually clears. Nobody notices when it goes right.

The elected part matters to me. I am handling money belonging to people who chose me to handle it, and I would rather be careful and slow than lose that.

02Current

Co-founder & Treasurer · Ethics Bowl club · ~15 members

Starting our Ethics Bowl club

After competing in the National High School Ethics Bowl and placing top ten in Northern California, five of us wanted the event to have a permanent home at Amador Valley instead of a team assembled from scratch every year. We started a club for it. It has grown to roughly fifteen members.

I track the finances and lead fundraising. For a club this new the second part is the whole job, because there is no budget to administer until somebody raises one. Starting something turns out to be a different skill from competing in it, and most of the founding work has been structure and persuasion rather than ethics.

Business plan · DECA Franchise Business Plan

Oakberry franchise plan

My partner and I were given a blank page and a brief: a complete twenty page plan for opening a franchise location, defensible enough that a judge could poke at any section of it.

We started with site selection and worked outward. We mapped the competition in the surrounding area, profiled the target market, and went through the product mix line by line asking what we would change to fit the people who actually live there. The marketing plan ran on two tracks, digital and on the ground. I owned the financial plan and built multi-year projections from the unit economics up.

First place at the Northern California conference and a top ten finish in California at states. The finance section held up best under questioning. The marketing plan was solid but conventional, which is the part I would rebuild.

04In progress

Research · Research Spark Hub

Credit risk in the age of AI

A mentored research paper on credit risk and the effect artificial intelligence is having on the field. Credit risk assessment has always been a judgment problem dressed up as a maths problem, and machine learning changes the balance between the two: models read more signal than any underwriter, but they also inherit whatever is buried in the training data.

I'm working through what that means for lenders, for borrowers, and particularly for people whose circumstances sit outside a model's assumptions. My mentor is Lavanya Prahlad. The paper is still being written.

Competition · DECA Business Law & Ethics, TDM

Business Law & Ethics

A team decision making event: you receive a legal scenario, get a short window to prepare with your partner, then present and defend a recommendation to a judge free to push back on any of it. You cannot memorise your way through it. The scenarios change and the judge follows up on whatever is weakest in your reasoning, so the only real preparation is understanding the doctrine well enough to apply it cold. Top six in California and an ICDC qualification.

Competition · National High School Ethics Bowl

Ethics Bowl

Ethics Bowl is not debate. You are not assigned a side and you do not win by beating the other team; you win by reasoning well, and judges reward you for conceding a good point. Top ten in the Northern California region. It made me a noticeably better listener, which carried straight back into my DECA roleplays, and it is the reason we later started a club for it.
