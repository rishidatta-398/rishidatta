01
Business Plan DECA Franchise Business Plan · February 2026

Oakberry franchise plan

My partner and I were given a blank page and a brief: produce a complete, twenty page plan for opening a franchise location, defensible enough that a judge could poke at any section of it.

We started with site selection, then worked outward. We mapped the competition in the surrounding area, profiled the target market, and went through the product and service mix line by line, asking what we would change to fit the people who actually live there. The marketing plan ran on two tracks, digital and on the ground. I owned the financial plan and built multi-year projections in Google Sheets from the unit economics up.

Result: first place at the Northern California Career Development Conference and a top ten finish in California at the state conference. The finance section held up best under questioning. The marketing plan was solid but conventional, which is the part I would rebuild if I did it again.

02
Research · In progress Research Spark Hub · 2026

Credit risk in the age of AI

A mentored research paper on credit risk and the effect artificial intelligence is having on the field. Credit risk assessment has always been a judgment problem dressed up as a math problem, and machine learning changes the balance between the two: models can read more signal than any underwriter, but they also inherit whatever is buried in the training data.

I'm working through what that means for lenders, for borrowers, and particularly for people whose circumstances sit outside a model's assumptions. My mentor is Lavanya Prahlad at Research Spark Hub. The paper is still being written.

03
Competition DECA Business Law & Ethics, TDM · 2026

Business Law & Ethics

A team decision making event: you receive a legal scenario, you get a short window to prepare with your partner, and then you present and defend a recommendation to a judge who is free to push back on any of it.

You cannot memorize your way through this one. The scenarios change and the judge follows up on whatever is weakest in your reasoning, so the only real preparation is understanding the underlying doctrine well enough to apply it to a situation you have never seen.

Result: top six in California and qualification for the International Career Development Conference.

Competition National High School Ethics Bowl · 2025

Ethics Bowl

Ethics Bowl is not debate. You are not assigned a side and you do not win by beating the other team, you win by reasoning well, and the judges reward you for conceding a good point. Top ten in the Northern California region. It made me a noticeably better listener, which carried straight back into my DECA roleplays.
